How we work

Our stories feature a recurring animated family (grandparents, parents and children) and are written, voiced and animated with the help of AI tools, then reviewed and approved by a person before they are published. Videos that show realistic AI-made people are labelled as AI-generated on each platform.

About our app

The ContentAdda app is our private publishing tool. It is used only by the ContentAdda team to upload our own approved videos to our own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts, and to read the statistics of those videos. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information about viewers. See our privacy policy.
