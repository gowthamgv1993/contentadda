Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what the ContentAdda app (our private publishing tool) and our channels do with information.

Who we are

ContentAdda is an independent content studio. Contact: contentadda.studio@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

The app is used only by the ContentAdda team with our own accounts. When we connect our own YouTube channels (through Google sign-in) and our own Instagram accounts (through Meta's Instagram Login), the app receives permission to:

  • upload videos we have approved to our own channels and accounts;
  • read basic information about our own channels and accounts (name, handle, number of videos);
  • read statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments counts, watch time).

The app cannot delete videos, manage comments, send messages or change channel settings, and it does not access any other person's account.

Viewers

The app does not collect, store or sell information about people who watch our videos. Viewer interactions on YouTube and Instagram are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies (Google, Instagram).

How we store access

Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own secured server, readable only by the studio's account, never shared with anyone and never published. They are used only for the purposes above.

Google API Services

ContentAdda's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access and data deletion

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram in the app under Settings → Apps and websites. To ask us to delete any data related to you, write to contentadda.studio@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes.
